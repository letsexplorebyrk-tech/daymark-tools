Diabetes Daily-Care Planner

This is an organiser only. Do not change treatment or interpret data from this page.

Living well with diabetes runs on routine - the daily tasks you and your clinician have already agreed on. This free planner keeps those tasks in one short checklist you can tick through each day, with a copy button that turns today's list into plain text for your records.

Open the free planner
No sign-up. Runs entirely in your browser.

What it does

You enter your own tasks - the routine you already follow - with a time or category note for each. The planner keeps them as a checklist saved in your browser, so tomorrow's routine is exactly where you left it. Tick items off as you go, and copy the day's summary whenever you want a record.

What it never does

No readings are interpreted here. The planner cannot tell you what a number means, what to eat, or whether your routine should change. It does not assess, score or advise. The routine in the list is the one you and your clinician agreed on - if it should change, that is a conversation with them, not an edit suggested by a tool.

Organise the routine you already use

Examples of checklist wording might be “pack the supplies on my clinician’s list” or “write down a question for my next visit”. Enter only tasks already agreed with your care team, and mark them as a personal planning aid.

Not a clinical monitor

The planner does not accept or interpret glucose readings, make treatment recommendations, or detect an emergency. Follow your existing care plan and contact a qualified professional for medical concerns.

Private by design

Health routines are nobody else's business. Everything you type stays in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared, ever.

FAQ

Is this daily-care planner really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier. It runs in your browser and costs nothing.

Can it interpret my readings or suggest changes?

No. The planner does not accept or interpret readings of any kind. It holds the routine tasks you and your clinician have agreed on - interpretation and changes belong with your care team.

Where is my routine stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing leaves your machine.

Can I keep a record of what I completed?

Yes. The copy-summary button turns your checklist into plain text you can paste into any notes app or message yourself.
