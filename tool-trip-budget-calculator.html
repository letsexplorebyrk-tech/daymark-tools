Trip Budget Calculator

Most trip budgets fail before the trip starts, because they are one optimistic number. This free calculator takes your own assumptions - distance, your vehicle's mileage, today's fuel price, stays, and a food-and-activities figure - and turns them into three scenarios: low, base and high. You see the range you are actually committing to, not a fantasy point estimate.

How it works

Enter five numbers you already know or can guess honestly: how many kilometres you will drive, your vehicle's kilometres per litre, the fuel price per litre, what you expect to pay for stays, and a combined figure for food, tolls and activities. The tool computes fuel cost from the first three, adds the rest, and presents low, base and high totals so you can stress-test the plan before you book anything.

Why three scenarios beat one estimate

Fuel prices move, stays get upgraded, and someone always wants the extra stop. A single-number budget breaks on first contact with the trip. A range tells you the worst case you are signing up for. If the high scenario still fits, the plan survives reality. If it does not, you fix the assumption now, while fixing is free.

Worked example: expose the assumptions

For 420 km at 14 km per litre and ₹105 per litre, fuel is ₹3,150. Add ₹3,500 for stays and ₹3,000 for food, tolls and activities: the base estimate is ₹9,650. The displayed lower and higher scenarios are mechanical multipliers of that base, not a forecast or quote. Change one input at a time to see which assumption matters most.

What this estimate leaves out

Enter round-trip distance if you want return fuel included. Add parking, repairs, local transport, insurance, or other costs into your own “other” figure. The calculator cannot check route length, live fuel prices, road conditions, or bookings.

Private by design

The calculator runs completely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, stored on a server, or shared. Close the tab and it is gone. That is how every Daymark tool works: useful planning, zero data collection.

FAQ

Is this trip budget calculator really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier. It runs in your browser and costs nothing.

Does it store my trip details?

No. Everything is calculated locally. Your numbers never leave your device.

What costs should I include?

Fuel (from distance, mileage and price), stays, food, tolls and activities. The inputs are separated so you can see which assumption moves the total most.

Why not just one number?

One number pretends to precision you do not have yet. Low, base and high scenarios show the range you are committing to, so surprises never break the plan.
