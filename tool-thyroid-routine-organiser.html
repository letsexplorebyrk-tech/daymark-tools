Thyroid Routine Organiser

This is an organiser only. Do not change treatment or interpret data from this page.

Managing a thyroid routine means remembering small things on a long cycle: the daily habit you already follow, the refill date, and the questions you meant to ask at the next appointment. This free organiser keeps all three in one private list that lives in your browser.

Open the free organiser
No sign-up. Runs entirely in your browser.

What it does

Add your own entries - a routine reminder, a refill date, or a question you want to raise with your clinician - each with a short note. The organiser keeps them as a checklist saved locally in your browser, and the copy button turns the list into plain text you can bring to an appointment or keep in your records.

What it never does

It does not advise. The organiser cannot tell you what your results mean, whether your routine is right, or when to take anything. It holds the information you enter so the questions you already have are ready when you sit down with your clinician - the answers come from them.

Turn questions into a visit note

Keep the refill date you already track, note a question you want to ask, and bring the list to your appointment. For example, separate “ask pharmacist about refill timing” from the date on the current prescription label.

Follow the source instructions

This tool does not advise on timing, food interactions, test results, or dose changes. Check medicine and testing instructions with your clinician or pharmacist; do not use a browser checklist as a substitute.

Private by design

Your routine and your health questions stay in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded or shared. That is not a setting - it is how the tool is built.

FAQ

Is this thyroid organiser really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier. It runs in your browser and costs nothing.

Does it interpret thyroid results or give advice?

No. It never interprets results and never advises. It stores your routine, refill date and questions - what those mean is a conversation with your clinician.

Where is my information stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Can I bring my questions to an appointment?

Yes. The copy-summary button puts your list on the clipboard as plain text, ready to paste into your phone's notes or a message.
