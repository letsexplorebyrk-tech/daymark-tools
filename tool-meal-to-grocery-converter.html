How it works

Copy the ingredient block from any recipe - a website, a message, a handwritten note you typed out - and paste it in. The tool reads each line, identifies the ingredient, and files it. One tap sends everything to the Daymark smart grocery list, where it lands in the right sections (kitchen or home) alongside anything already on your list.

Built for Indian kitchens

Most converters understand flour and sugar and stop there. This one knows the difference a well-stocked Indian kitchen cares about: dals, atta, poha, haldi, jeera, rai. Paste a recipe the way it is actually written and the list still comes out right.

Worked example: “tomato, onion, ginger”

Paste one item per line or separate items with commas. Common catalogue matches are grouped into likely shop sections; unmatched words are kept as text so you can review them. Select Recognise & sort, check the result, then choose Send to grocery only when the items look right.

Review before adding

This tool does not parse full recipes, infer serving sizes, or calculate quantities. “Chilli powder to taste” may remain an unmatched note. Remove anything you do not want and add quantities in the grocery list.

Private by design

The conversion happens entirely in your browser. Your recipes and your grocery list never touch a server - they stay in your device's local storage. No account, no tracking of what your household cooks.

FAQ

Common Indian kitchen items - dals, atta, rice, spices like haldi and jeera - alongside everyday English names.

Where does the list go?

Straight into the Daymark smart grocery list on your device, sorted into sections and ready for the shop.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier.

No. It reads ingredient lines and files them. Quantities and cooking decisions stay yours.
