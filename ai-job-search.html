Free AI Job Search Tools and Prompts

Most people open an AI chat, type "help me find a job", and get a generic answer. The fix is not a better AI - it is a better brief. These free prompts turn a blank chat into a task with inputs, limits and a defined output, so the assistant can actually tailor a resume, draft recruiter outreach, or run a mock interview. Copy them, fill in the brackets, paste them into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

Three free prompts to start with

Tailor a resume to one job post.
"Here is my resume: [paste resume]. Here is the job description: [paste job post]. List the 8 keywords the job post repeats, show which ones my resume already proves with evidence, and rewrite my 3 weakest bullet points to match the missing keywords. Keep every claim true to what I actually did."
Recruiter outreach that is not spam.
"Write a 90-word LinkedIn message to a recruiter at [company] for the [role] opening. Reference one specific thing about the company's work from [paste a recent post or job line], state my matching experience in one sentence, and end with a yes-or-no question they can answer in one word."
Mock interview with honest scoring.
"Act as the hiring manager for [role] at [company]. Ask me one question at a time, starting with the most common screening question for this role. After each of my answers, score it out of 10, name the one thing that would make it stronger, then ask the next question. Do not go easy on me."
