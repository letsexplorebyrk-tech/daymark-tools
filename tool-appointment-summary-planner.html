Appointment Summary Planner

This is an organiser only. Do not change treatment or interpret data from this page.

Appointments go better when you walk in prepared: the questions you want answered, the documents you need to bring, and a short summary of what to cover. This free planner helps you collect all three beforehand and turn them into a clean, copyable summary you can carry in on your phone.

Open the free planner
No sign-up. Runs entirely in your browser.

What it does

Add each question or document as an entry with a short note - a topic, a date, anything that helps. The planner keeps them as a checklist saved in your browser. Before you leave, tick what is ready and copy the summary as plain text. After the appointment, update the list with follow-ups while they are fresh.

What it never does

It does not suggest what to ask or interpret anything about your care. The questions in the list are yours - the planner just makes sure they are written down and with you when the appointment starts, instead of remembered in the car park.

Prepare a short, useful summary

Before a visit, list the questions you want answered, the documents you plan to bring, and reminders you already received. Group one point per line so it is easy to scan, then copy or print the notes for your own use.

Protect private details

Use a device you control and clear the local list when appropriate. The planner does not send the summary to a clinic, create a medical record, or replace urgent care or professional advice.

Private by design

Your questions and notes stay in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared. Preparing for an appointment is personal; the tool keeps it that way.

FAQ

Is this appointment planner really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier. It runs in your browser and costs nothing.

Does it suggest questions or give medical guidance?

No. It stores the questions and reminders you write yourself. It never suggests what to ask and never interprets anything about your care.

Where are my notes stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I carry the summary with me?

The copy-summary button puts your list on the clipboard as plain text. Paste it into your phone's notes or any message, and it is with you at the appointment.
