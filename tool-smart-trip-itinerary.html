Smart Trip Itinerary

A trip lives or dies by the day plan. Stops scattered across notes and screenshots turn into backtracking, missed timings and the sinking 4 PM realisation that the best stop is now on the wrong side of town. This free planner groups your stops by day into a clean, map-ready itinerary - and it asks for your location only if you choose to use it for directions.

How it works

Add your stops and assign them to days. The planner keeps each day's route together so the plan reads the way the trip will actually run: morning to evening, one sensible line per day. When you are ready to move, open the day in your maps app. If you want directions from where you are standing, that is the single moment the tool asks for your location - for that purpose only.

Planning with reversible assumptions

Plans change: a museum closes, rain moves in, someone sleeps late. A day-grouped plan absorbs that. Swap stops between days in seconds and the trip still holds its shape, because the structure is days and routes, not a fragile minute-by-minute schedule.

Example: a two-day stop list

Add a station arrival to Day 1, then a hotel and museum with approximate times. Put the next morning’s destination under Day 2. Use the map buttons when ready to open the last stop’s directions; location access is requested only if you choose the location button.

Keep the plan provisional

Times and stop order are notes you enter, not live traffic or opening-hour checks. Confirm addresses, operating hours, and travel conditions with the provider. You can still use manual destinations if you decline location access.

Private by design

Your itinerary lives in your browser's local storage, on your device, and nowhere else. No account, no sync server, no record of where you plan to go. Close the tab and come back later; the plan is still on your phone, not on our servers.

FAQ

Does it track my location?

No. The plan is built from stops you enter. Your location is requested only if you tap for directions, and used only for that.

Is my trip plan saved?

It stays in your browser's local storage on your device, surviving a closed tab but never touching a server.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier.

What does map-ready mean?

Stops grouped by day in your chosen order, so each day reads as a clean route to open in your maps app.
