Medication Schedule Organiser

This is an organiser only. Do not change treatment or interpret data from this page.

If you already have a medication routine agreed with your clinician, the hard part is keeping the schedule and refill dates in one place you actually check. This free organiser stores your existing schedule - the names, times and refill dates you enter yourself - as a simple list with a one-tap summary you can copy anywhere.

Open the free organiser
No sign-up. Runs entirely in your browser.

What it does

You add your own entries: a medication name or reminder, and a schedule note such as a time or a refill date. The organiser keeps them as a checklist you can tick off and copy as a plain-text summary for a family member or your records. It saves your list locally in your browser, so it is there the next time you open the page.

What it never does

Never dose advice. The organiser does not tell you what to take, how much, or when. It does not check interactions, suggest changes, or interpret anything you enter. Those decisions belong to you and your clinician. If a reminder looks wrong, the fix is a call to your pharmacist or doctor, not an edit based on a website.

Keep a reminder factual

Write down the schedule exactly as already directed by your clinician or pharmacist, then use the list to keep a refill date or question in one place. Check each saved entry against the original label or care instructions before relying on it.

Important limits

This page does not set alarms, verify that a dose was taken, identify medicines, recommend a dose, or warn about interactions. Do not use it to change treatment. Keep sensitive information off shared devices.

Private by design

Everything you enter stays in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared - which matters for health information more than anything else you plan online. Close the tab and clear your browser data, and it is gone.

FAQ

Is this medication organiser really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier. It runs in your browser and costs nothing.

Does it give dose advice or check my medications?

No - never. It stores the schedule you already have and nothing more. It cannot tell you what to take, how much, or whether two medications interact. Those questions are for your clinician or pharmacist.

Where is my schedule stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so your health notes stay private by default.

Can I share my list with a family member?

Yes. The copy-summary button puts your list on the clipboard as plain text, so you can paste it into any message or note yourself. The tool never sends anything anywhere.
