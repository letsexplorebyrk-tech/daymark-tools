Packing List Builder

The night-before pack is where trips go wrong: you either dump half the cupboard into the bag or forget the charger, the medicines and the one document you needed at the gate. This free builder starts you from a profile that already knows the trip type - weekend, monsoon or family - and lets you edit it down to exactly your list. You pack from the list, tick as you go, and leave with nothing forgotten and nothing dead-weight.

How it works

Pick the profile closest to your trip. The tool loads a sensible starting list for that trip type - the monsoon profile remembers the umbrella and the quick-dry extras, the family profile remembers the things that are painful to buy at a destination with kids in tow. Then you edit: remove what does not apply, add what only you would think of, and tick items off as they go into the bag.

Why profiles beat blank lists

A blank list depends on your memory at 11 PM, which is exactly when memory is least reliable. A profile flips the work: instead of recalling items, you reject them. Rejecting is fast and almost error-proof, which is why profile-based lists catch the things blank lists miss.

Make a profile fit this trip

A weekend profile may remind you about a charger and toiletries; a monsoon profile may start with a waterproof bag and umbrella. These are prompts, not a complete packing recommendation. Add personal essentials, remove unsuitable items, and mark packed items as you go.

Check the details that vary

Trip length, weather, airline baggage limits, local rules, and access to laundry all change what makes sense. Confirm documents and tickets with the relevant provider. Daymark does not know your itinerary or check local conditions.

Private by design

Your list is stored in your browser's local storage, on your device, and nowhere else. No account, no sync, no one mining your travel habits. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, the list is still there - on your phone, not on our servers.

FAQ

What profiles are included?

Weekend, monsoon and family. Each is a starting list you edit - add, remove and tick off as you pack.

Is my list saved?

It stays in your browser's local storage on your device. It survives a closed tab and never touches a server.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier.

Can I start from scratch?

Yes. Start from the closest profile and edit freely, or clear it and add your own items.
