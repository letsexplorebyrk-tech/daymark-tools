Grocery Basket Budget

The most expensive moment in grocery shopping is the checkout surprise - the total that lands fifty rupees past what felt right. This free tool moves that moment earlier. You enter your own price estimates for the basket, it totals them against your budget, and you see while still at home which items tip the week over.

How it works

Set the week's budget, then list your items with the price you expect each to cost. The tool keeps a running total and shows the gap - under or over - so the trade-offs happen in the list, not at the till. Swap the branded item for the staple, drop the impulse add, watch the total come back under the line.

Your estimates, honestly labelled

This tool makes no live price claims. Prices change by shop, brand and week, and any app promising exact totals is guessing with your money. What you get instead is a clean check of your own expectations against your own budget - which is usually enough to catch the basket that was quietly drifting over.

Example: compare a planned shop with your limit

Enter your own monthly limit, then add estimates such as rice ₹450, produce ₹700, and cleaning items ₹300. The total is ₹1,450 and the remaining amount is calculated against your stated limit. Edit or remove any line when your estimate changes.

Not a price checker

Prices, pack sizes, taxes, and availability are not fetched from shops. Use current local prices you have checked yourself; a remaining balance is arithmetic, not a recommendation to buy.

Private by design

Your budget and basket stay in your browser's local storage, on your device. No account, no server, no one building a profile of what your household spends. Close the tab; the numbers stay on your phone.

FAQ

Where do the prices come from?

From you. No live price claims - you enter your estimates and the tool checks the total against your budget.

Is my budget data saved?

It stays in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier.

Who is this for?

Anyone who wants to know before checkout whether this week's basket fits the budget - and which items tip it over.
